# BibleRank Facebook Post Ideas — Virality Angle

Lead with a hook people would engage with on its own merits (a question, a debate, a relatable moment). The site is the payoff, not the pitch.

## 1. The Debate Starter
**Post:** "Unpopular opinion: your favorite Bible verse says a lot about you.

Drop yours in the comments. Let's see what this group actually believes in. 👇"

**Weave-in (comment or follow-up, not the main post):** "We turned this into an actual leaderboard btw — bible-rank.com. Go put yours on top."

*Why it works:* comment bait first, no ask up front. The link rides in on the engagement it earns.

---

## 2. The Screenshot Bait
**Post:** A screenshot of the current #1 verse on the leaderboard, no caption context — just:

"This is currently the internet's #1 Bible verse. Agree or disagree?"

**Weave-in:** small caption underneath — "ranked live at bible-rank.com"

*Why it works:* looks like a stat/fact post, not an ad. People argue in the comments, which is the whole game.

---

## 3. The Tag-a-Friend Duel
**Post:** "Psalm 23 or Philippians 4:13 — which one actually gets you through hard days?

Tag someone who'd pick the other one."

**Weave-in:** "Settle it for real → bible-rank.com/duel"

*Why it works:* forces a tag/share to resolve the disagreement, which is the actual growth mechanic. Site link is the tiebreaker, not the intro.

---

## 4. The Relatable Confession
**Post:** "Be honest — do you actually know the verse you have tattooed / on your wall / as your phone background? Or did you just like how it sounded?

No judgment. Just curious how many of us are winging it. 😅"

**Weave-in (reply to top comments):** "if you want to actually look it up (and back it) — bible-rank.com"

*Why it works:* self-deprecating, relatable, doesn't mention the site until people are already in the thread.

---

## 5. The Number Nobody Expects
**Post:** "[X] people have put real money behind a single Bible verse this month.

Guess which one before I tell you."

**Weave-in:** reveal in the comments a few hours later with the link — "Here's the answer → bible-rank.com/leaderboard"

*Why it works:* curiosity gap + delayed reveal keeps the post alive longer and drives a second wave of comments when you post the answer.

---

## 6. The "Would You Rather" Bible Edition
**Post:**

"Would you rather have one of these tattooed on you for life? No skipping. 👇

🅰️ Psalm 91:11 — 'For he will put his angels in charge of you, to guard you in all your ways.'
*For the ones who need to feel protected more than they need to feel understood.*

🅱️ Romans 8:28 — 'We know that all things work together for good for those who love God, to those who are called according to his purpose.'
*For the ones who need everything to make sense before they can rest.*

Drop your letter below."

**Weave-in:** "There's a whole leaderboard of verses fighting for #1 if you want more of these → bible-rank.com"

*Why it works:* classic shareable game format, Scripture as the twist. Feels native to the feed, not promotional.

---

### Notes
- None of these open with "BibleRank is..." — the brand shows up after the hook has already earned attention (in-comment, in a reply, or as a small caption).
- Best performers to screenshot for these: current #1 verse, a live duel matchup, and the site's visitor/contribution stats from /about.
- Rotate formats (debate, tag-a-friend, would-you-rather, curiosity gap) so the page doesn't read as one repeated template.
