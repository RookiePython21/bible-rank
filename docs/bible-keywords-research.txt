KEYWORD DIFFICULTY IS MISSING
  The Ahrefs `difficulty` column returns "internal server error" on this API
  key. It was retried roughly a dozen ways — alone, batched, at tiny result
  limits, with extended timeouts — and succeeded only twice out of many
  attempts. Volume, CPC, and global volume are all reliable. Re-run the KD
  pull later, or read it from the Ahrefs UI.

THE 500 CEILING IS AN ARTIFACT
  The "bible verses about X" pattern has well over 100 terms sitting at
  exactly 500/mo. The 20 in Cluster 1 are a relevance-based selection, not an
  exhaustive top-20. A long tail is available for a larger content plan.
